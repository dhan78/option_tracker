Dealer Gamma (GEX)
Top: net dealer gamma per strike ($mm per 1% move). Bottom: total GEX as spot itself moves — the dome above the flip = long gamma (dips bought, vol suppressed, pins to walls); the dip below = short gamma (dips sold, moves amplified). Convention: dealers long calls / short puts; T-1 open interest.
Charm = delta decay between now and today's 4pm close, with spot & IV held fixed. As theta bleeds, option deltas drift and dealers must re-hedge: green bars = they buy (upward pull toward that strike), red = they sell. The badge sums it — a positive $mm total is the mechanical into-close pin drift up. Strongest near expiring OTM strikes; fades to zero at the bell.
